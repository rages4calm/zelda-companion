Treasure Shuffle. Original additions use the [MIT license](LICENSE).

## Native foundation

- **snesrev and elzo_d** — [zelda3 native engine](https://github.com/snesrev/zelda3).
  MIT source with separate bundled dependency notices. See
  [engine/LICENSE.txt](engine/LICENSE.txt).
- **RadzPrower** — [Zelda 3 Launcher](https://github.com/RadzPrower/Zelda-3-Launcher),
  the initial setup tool. Its locally repaired source is retained under
  `repaired-launcher/`, with its [MIT license](repaired-launcher/LICENSE).
- **Hyllian and Zenju** — xBRZ freescale shader and scaling work. Original notices
  and shader source are in `shaders/xbrz/` and `Licenses/`; MIT/GPLv3 terms apply
  to their respective work.
- **SDL, Opus, Python, Pillow, PyYAML and .NET contributors** — graphics/input,
  audio, local extraction and Windows launcher runtimes. Their notices accompany
  the release and are retained where their source is vendored.
- **Cloudflare contributors** — unmodified `cloudflared` tunnel helper. Apache
  2.0 license, copyright notices, pinned version and checksum are in `Tools/`.
