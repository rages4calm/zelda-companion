# Update without losing progress

Keep the initialized portable folder as your personal installation. The new clean
ZIP is software; it does not include your saves, extracted assets or music.

1. Save normally in the game, then close the game and launcher.
2. Make a backup copy of your entire current portable folder.
3. Extract the new clean ZIP into a **new** writable folder.
4. Copy these personal items from the old folder to the new one:
   - `Game/zelda3_assets.dat` and `Game/saves/`.
   - `Game/zelda3.ini` for controls/display/audio and advanced settings.
   - The entire `UserData/` folder for campaigns, seeds, imported music and preferences.
5. Open the **new** top-level `Zelda Companion.exe`. Do not run the old copy.

The new folder keeps its new software and runtime files. Do not replace its whole
Game/ or Relay/ folders with old ones. Leave your backup intact until you have
verified your original adventure and continued campaign.

For co-op, everyone updates before reconnecting. Select the correct adventure
first, then its saved campaign. A recipe reproduces treasure and does not transfer
progress. Save-state rewinds may diverge from friends' progress.

Friends receive the clean release ZIP, not your initialized or backed-up folder.
