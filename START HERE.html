Zelda Companion 0.3.1 · portable Windows x64 community edition

Open Zelda Companion.exe

It is beside this guide, at the top level of the extracted folder.
Game/ and Relay/ contain supporting programs.

First: right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Keep every folder together. Required runtimes and tools are already included.

First Launch

1. Right-click the ZIP -> Extract All into a writable folder.
Keep all folders together. Do not run from inside the ZIP.
2. Open Zelda Companion.exe. Runtimes and tools are bundled.
3. On Play, choose your own original, unpatched USA .sfc or .smc ROM.
Assets are generated locally. No ROM is provided or downloaded.
4. Choose a look. Smooth 1080p is recommended. Click Launch solo.
5. Guide in the sidebar (or F1 in the launcher) explains setup and controls.

Default Controls

Arrows: move. Z: sword. X: action/dash. A: equipped tool. S: map.
Enter: inventory/start. Right Shift: select/save menu. Alt+Enter: fullscreen.
Settings -> Configure controls opens the visual editor for keyboard and controller.
Choose a category, click a binding, then press the key or button you want.
Hold Ctrl/Alt/Shift for keyboard combinations. For a controller combination,
hold the first button, press the second and release both. Save controls applies
changes on the next game launch and backs up the previous settings.
Restore defaults resets the draft; Discard changes leaves saved controls alone.
Connect a compatible USB/Bluetooth controller; its name and live input appear.
Button labels use Xbox names. The left stick also moves Link. Each online player
can use different controls. No text-file editing is required for remapping.
Current game mappings appear in Guide -> Controls & saving.
Save in the game before closing its window. Then close the launcher.

Upgraded Music (Optional)

Choose Music -> Import ZIP for an A Link to the Past MSU-1 audio pack.
Or extract a .7z/.rar pack and use Import folder. Only one track set per import.
Find music packs opens the creator-listed Zeldix catalogue.
Choose the imported soundtrack, then Use this soundtrack. Music volume controls
its balance against game sound effects. Missing tracks use original music.
Leave MSU Deluxe off unless the author specifies an extended Deluxe pack.
No ROM patch, intro-video file, file renaming or INI editing is needed.
Changes apply on the next launch. Close the game before changing music.
Original SNES music switches back; imported packs remain in your library.
Packs live in UserData/Soundtracks and work in both solo and online campaigns.
Each player can use different music. Settings are backed up when saving a choice.
The clean software ZIP includes no downloaded soundtrack; friends add their own.

Randomizer: Treasure Shuffle

Set up your original USA ROM, then choose Randomizer in the sidebar.
Enter a number or phrase, choose Balanced or Surprise, then Generate seed.
Play selected seed starts an adventure with its own saves. Play -> Launch solo
still opens your original game. The optional checkbox includes chest heart pieces.
Progression protection is ALWAYS ON: required equipment, all keys, maps,
compasses, big chests, bombs, arrows, entrances and story events stay original.
Only rupee and optional heart-piece chest rewards move. Reward totals stay intact.
For friends: Export recipe, send the .z3seed.json file, and have them Import recipe.
Everyone uses their own USA game data. Choose Use for online and the same seed.
Different seeds are refused; each seed keeps separate online campaigns and saves.
These native seeds are not full ALttPR seeds. ALttPR patched ROMs are incompatible.
A full randomized campaign has not been played through. See RANDOMIZER.md and
Guide -> Randomizer & progression for the scope and verified protections.

Up To Eight Players Over The Internet (Including Starlink)

Everyone needs 0.3.1, their own ROM and matching adventure/look/gameplay settings,
shared-progress and combat choices. Older two-player releases cannot join.
Host: Online -> Host a room -> Internet · automatic -> enter your name.
Choose a new or continued online campaign, then Create room & play.
Copy invitation and privately send it to friends. Keep the host launcher open.
Friend: Online -> Join a room -> enter name, relay address and room code.
Select your online campaign, match the invitation's choices, then Join room & play.
No account, router changes, DMZ, port forwarding, VPN or GitHub tool is needed.
Cloudflare carries co-op traffic. This free testing service has no uptime guarantee.
Closing the host's connection ends the link; create a new invitation to reconnect.
Meet in the same area, world and dungeon floor. Names are shown above the Links.
Eight slot colors: green, blue, red, purple, gold, cyan, rose and silver.
The roster lists players and where they are. Show player names is a personal option.
Same network / LAN is optional for one network. Permanent relays: README.md.

Continue Your Online Adventure

Online saves are separate from solo saves. Save normally before closing.
When a room expires, create/join a new room and choose Continue in the campaign
selector. Each player selects their own corresponding adventure. Dates help
identify saved campaigns; the last choice is remembered. Saves are backed up
before launch. Room expiration does not erase your campaign.

Co-Op Features & Limits

Share equipment & campaign progress merges permanent tools, gear upgrades,
empty bottle ownership, health capacity/piece progress, maps/compasses/big keys,
pendants, crystals, explored areas, persistent doors and story milestones.
Received progress stays in the online save. A shared lantern is in inventory
after dialogue closes; it does not force a held/equipped animation.
Current health, magic, ammo, rupees, small keys, chest and bottle contents,
selected tool and local saves stay personal. Heart totals converge to the
furthest collected total; different simultaneous pieces are not added together.
Opening scripts and water-reset exceptions protect gameplay. Some world changes
need an area re-entry. Stay at similar progression and use trusted friends.
Shared enemy combat is optional and OFF by default. It synchronizes 32 ordinary
enemy types in the same area/floor. Bosses, NPCs, puzzles and projectiles stay
independent; spawns and timing may differ and movement may jitter.
This is not complete campaign-wide multiplayer. No PvP, chat, player collision,
reliable depth occlusion, anti-cheat or automatic reconnect.
Guide -> Co-op features & limits explains details. Validation.txt records tests.
Eight actual local game processes and eight public WSS clients were tested.
The older release connected Carl and a friend. This new release still needs
a long campaign and multiple physical-computer test.

Saves, Privacy And Sharing

Original solo saves: Game/saves. Original online saves: UserData/Online.
Treasure Shuffle solo saves: UserData/Randomizer/<seed ID>/Game/saves.
Seeded online saves: UserData/Online/Seeds/<seed ID>. All are backed up before launch.
Save normally before closing; unsaved progress isn't guaranteed.
Room codes grant access to open slots. Keep them private. They expire when empty.
Use the experiment with people you trust. No anti-cheat or automatic reconnect.
Share the CLEAN release ZIP, never an initialized folder: it contains generated
Nintendo assets and may contain saves. Every friend supplies their own ROM.
Unsigned apps may trigger SmartScreen. Verify the source and checksum.
Do not disable security software globally.
