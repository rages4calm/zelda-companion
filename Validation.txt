Coverage limits:
This is a short functional test, not a full playthrough or a performance benchmark.
It did not use two physical computers or packet-loss injection. The older native
bot recording used loopback; the newer public WSS test routes both clients through
Cloudflare's Internet edge. Tool tests validate the native memory grant path, not a
two-person campaign with every item pickup. Normal campaign save persistence and
every story transition still need longer gameplay testing. Full world/combat/story
sync is intentionally absent and is not claimed as passing.
